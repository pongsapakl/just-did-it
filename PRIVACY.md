# Privacy Policy

**Just Did It does not collect any data.**

Last updated: 20 August 2026

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — your list of actions and groups, which days you checked
them off, and which days you opened the app — is stored only on your device, in
the app's own private storage. Your reminder preference and the recent times of
day you opened the app are stored alongside it, and are used solely to decide
when to show your daily reminder.

None of this is transmitted anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No servers. The app has no networking code of any kind and never connects to
  the internet.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
- No third-party SDKs.
- Nothing is shared with the developer or with anyone else.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your
device. These are generated entirely on the device and are not sent through any
server.

## Retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the
app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else for anyone to retrieve or
delete on your behalf.

If the app is installed through TestFlight, Apple separately collects its own
information about testers and app usage under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); that is outside
this app's control.
